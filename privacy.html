EN

Privacy policy — 1 November 2026

DuoTris works offline. The app does not collect or transmit your personal data, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. No account is required.

Scores, records, language and settings are stored locally on your device. They remain until you delete the app and its data. Offloading the app keeps its data. iOS may include app data in your device backups; you manage those backups in your Apple settings. We have no access to them.

Game controllers use Apple's GameController framework. DuoTris does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or location. Music playback does not record audio.

If you email support, your address and the content you send are handled through Gmail to answer your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle your request; you can request its deletion at the address below. Gmail's own service terms and privacy policy apply to email processing.

Contact for support and privacy: contact.duotris@gmail.com

This policy describes this version of the app. Any change to data handling will be reflected in an updated policy.
